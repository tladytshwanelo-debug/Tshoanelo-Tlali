TOP PLACES TO VISIT

Maletsunyane Falls

Maletsunyane Falls is one of the highest single-drop waterfalls in Africa. It falls about 192 metres into a beautiful gorge near Semonkong.

Tshehlanyane National Park

Tshehlanyane National Park is one of Lesotho's beautiful national parks. It has high mountains, deep river valleys and different types of plants and wildlife.

Thaba Bosiu

Thaba Bosiu is an important historical place in Lesotho. It served as a mountain stronghold for King Moshoeshoe I and played an important role in the history of the Basotho nation.

Bokong National Reserve

Bokong Nature Reserve is a beautiful high-altitude protected area in Lesotho. It is famous for its mountains, waterfalls, wetlands, birds and amazing views.
